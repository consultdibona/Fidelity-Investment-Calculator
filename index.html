6. Learn about investing

What does high investment risk mean?

High risk means the value could change a lot, including dropping right before you want the money. If $100 falls 30%, it becomes $70. It may recover later, but there's no guarantee when.

Investor.gov — Stocks ↗
How are stocks different from bonds?

Buying stock means owning a tiny piece of a company. Buying a bond means lending money to a government or company. Stocks can grow more over long periods but can fall sharply. Bonds pay interest but can still lose value or default.

Investor.gov — Bonds ↗
Why invest in funds instead of individual companies?

A fund can own hundreds or thousands of investments. Spreading money around helps avoid betting everything on one company, but it does not eliminate losses when markets decline.

Investor.gov — Diversification ↗
What is a money market fund?

A money market mutual fund holds short-term debt securities and usually aims for a stable $1 share value. It pays a changing income yield. It is not an FDIC-insured savings account and could lose money.

Investor.gov — Funds ↗
What happens when a target-maturity ETF ends?

IBDU is designed to wind down in December 2029 and return its remaining net assets. That is not the same as guaranteeing the original investment. Its price can change and company bonds can default.
